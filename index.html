About Our Los Angeles Event Venue

A blank canvas built for bold ideas.

Epic Icon Center is a private event and creative production space designed for invite-only gatherings, brand activations, rehearsals, workshops, photo/film shoots, and private celebrations.

  • Private events & invite-only gatherings
  • Brand activations & pop-ups (private access)
  • Photo & film production
  • Rehearsals, showcases & creative workshops
  • Corporate meetings & private celebrations

Our flexible, industrial-style warehouse provides a controlled environment for pre-scheduled private bookings. All events are by reservation only and hosted for private groups and invited guests.

Epic Icon Center is a premier creative event venue located in the heart of Los Angeles. Designed as a versatile, open-concept space, the venue offers a refined yet flexible environment ideal for hosting a wide range of events and productions.

With approximately 6,000 square feet of open floor space, high ceilings, and a clean, modern aesthetic, Epic Icon Center serves as a blank canvas that can be transformed to match your vision. Whether you are planning a private celebration, corporate event, brand activation, pop-up, or film production, the space adapts seamlessly to both intimate gatherings and large-scale experiences.

The venue features white walls, customizable lighting, air conditioning throughout, and production-friendly access for easy load-in and load-out. Additional amenities include VIP parking, on-site concierge support, security options, and a variety of furniture and production add-ons to enhance your event.

Epic Icon Center is designed for creatives, innovators, brands, and visionaries seeking a stylish, professional venue with the flexibility to bring bold ideas to life.
